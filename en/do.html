Step 4 of 5

Carry out

Live your internship experience and learn by doing.

The attendance register

Fill in the register as you go on TSP, in the Periodi di tirocinio (internship periods) section: enter the hours you have done and check those still to do.

Save and submit

At the end of the internship the register must be saved and submitted. If it stays «In bozza» (draft), the organisation's tutor cannot see it or approve it.

  • Once the organisation's tutor approves it, the status becomes «Approvato» (approved).
  • At the end of the internship the register can be exported as a PDF.

When the internship is over

The internship is over when you have reached the total number of hours and/or the end date stated in the Training Project. At that point complete and submit the register and move on to step 5.

Extensions and interruptions

While the internship is active, you will find the Proroghe interruzioni (extensions/interruptions) button on TSP. An extension must be requested by the organisation before the end date of the internship: if you think you need one, talk to your tutor in good time.

What the organisation does meanwhile

  • Approves the attendance register.
  • Fills in the monitoring questionnaire.
  • Prepares the final report, available from 5 days before the end of the internship.

The internship cannot be closed without the organisation's final report: as the end approaches, check with your tutor that it is ready.

If something goes wrong

  • Problems with the platform: the University Internship Office (Settore Orientamento al lavoro e tirocini), tirocini@uniroma1.it.
  • Questions about your path: your academic tutor, or internship.dss@uniroma1.it.
