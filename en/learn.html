How many types of internship are there? Which one do you want to activate?

Curricular

Done during your studies to earn the credits (CFU) foreseen in your study plan. It must be completed before you graduate.

Extracurricular

For students and for graduates within 24 months of graduation at the internship start date.

Internship and thesis

If you want to write your thesis in collaboration with an external organisation, talk to your supervisor and your degree programme first.

Abroad

For students and for graduates within 18 months, activated through the TSP platform. See the Sapienza page and the instructions for international students.

Erasmus

If you are on an Erasmus study exchange you cannot also do an internship.

Are you an SMA student?

  • For Official Statistics students the curricular internship is compulsory and worth 9 CFU. It is recognised under exam code AAF1900 ("Tirocinio") or AAF1179 ("Per stages e tirocini presso imprese, enti pubblici o privati, ordini professionali").
  • For students of the other curricula the curricular internship is not compulsory, but strongly recommended.
  • SMA students must also write a final report at the end: see step 5.
