Where do I find companies?

  • Internship Day: the annual event of the Department of Statistical Sciences where companies and organisations meet students. See the recent editions on the DSS website.
  • Career Days: the University's main event for meeting employers (info); see also the Career Service.
  • Fellow students and professors: ask around, the best opportunities often come from here.
  • Public calls: some institutions, such as ISTAT, publish periodic calls. Other calls from public bodies and institutions are here.

Many other companies and institutions can host interns all year round.

Search on TSP

After logging in to TSP:

  1. Prepare your CVFill in your curriculum in the dedicated area: you need it to apply.
  2. Search organisations and opportunitiesIn the «Ricerca opportunità» (opportunity search) section you find projects already proposed by organisations: you can filter by keyword and see, for each offer, the details and the eligible degree programmes and subject areas. The host organisation search instead tells you whether the organisation you are interested in is already accredited: if not, it will have to get accredited (see Formalise).

Find your academic tutor

The academic tutor is the Sapienza lecturer who follows the internship from the academic side. Contact them before activating the internship: you will need to give their name to the DSS delegate when the Training Project is approved. It is also good practice to inform your degree programme before you start.

Internship Day: an opportunity

Every year the Department of Statistical Sciences organises the Internship Day, where companies and organisations meet students. The event promotes internships and opportunities for master's students to write their thesis in collaboration with external organisations. For information write to internship.dss@uniroma1.it or to the members of the Internship Committee.
