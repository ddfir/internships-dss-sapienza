For organisations and companies

Hosting an intern

To host interns you need to register on the TSP platform and sign an Agreement (Convenzione) with Sapienza, which includes insurance cover for the intern.

Agreement via TSP (recommended)

The Agreement makes your organisation visible as «convenzionato» (under agreement) to all students searching the platform and allows you to host interns.

  1. Register on TSPfollowing the instructions on signing the agreement (PDF, in Italian).
  2. Send the accreditation requestfrom «Accreditation Request». The platform can be switched to English.

Agreement by email

If you cannot use TSP, in particular as a foreign organisation, send a draft Agreement to the central Internship Office: viviana.adriani@uniroma1.it (cc tirocini@uniroma1.it and internship.dss@uniroma1.it; for SMA students also sma-dss@uniroma1.it). The Office assesses whether it can be signed and identifies who is authorised to sign it (the Rector or a delegate, such as the Head of the DSS).

Once the internship is active

  • Accept the student's application and create the Training Project with the «Avvio tirocinio diretto» (direct internship start) function: the student accepts it and the DSS delegate approves it; then everyone signs.
  • Your tutor approves the attendance register that the student submits on TSP.
  • Fill in the monitoring questionnaire and the final report (Internship Conclusion Statement) on TSP. The report is available from 5 days before the end of the internship.
  • The report must be signed by your tutor: the student downloads it from TSP and sends it, with the attendance register, to the lecturer who records the credits.
  • If the internship needs to be extended or interrupted, the request must be submitted before the end date.
