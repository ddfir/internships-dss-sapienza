Step 5 of 5

Validate

To get your credits, the internship must be formally closed on TSP and the final documents must reach the lecturer who records them.

Step by step (curricular internship)

  1. Submit the attendance registerCheck that it is saved and submitted. The organisation's tutor approves it on TSP.
  2. The organisation fills in the final reportThe organisation's tutor or contact person fills it in on TSP from 5 days before the end of the internship, together with the monitoring questionnaire.
  3. Fill in the evaluation questionnaireYou find it on TSP: it is separate from the final report and only you can see it, not the organisation.
  4. Download the PDFs from TSPFrom the Valutazione (evaluation) section you can download the final report and the approved attendance register as PDFs.
  5. If you are an SMA student, write your student reportSee below.
  6. Send the documents to the recording lecturerSend the signed Training Project (the DSS delegate sent it to you at activation), the approved attendance register and the final report signed by the organisation's tutor; SMA students add their own report. Always follow your degree programme's procedure. You can find the lecturers' contacts here.
  7. Register for the exam on InfostudAs soon as exam sessions are published, then wait for the result: at that point the credits are yours.
What the final report PDF is called

In the Valutazione section the final report filled in by the organisation downloads as riepilogoQuestionario-2.pdf (the name is set automatically by TSP; it is sometimes labelled «Attestazione finale»). The documents also remain available to the DSS delegate on TSP.

When the internship is «Concluso»

Once all final steps are completed, the Training Project automatically changes to «Concluso» (completed) and the internship is closed on TSP.

SMA only: your report

For the recognition of the credits for AAF1149, AAF1152, AAF1809 and AAF1965, after the internship is closed write a report:

  • Word document, maximum 5 A4 pages, with a fixed structure (see the template on the SMA Internships page);
  • signed by you and by the organisation's tutor;
  • to be sent to sma-dss@uniroma1.it together with the signed Training Project and the dated final report / Internship Conclusion Statement;
  • for the Official Statistics curriculum send it instead to roberta.varriale@uniroma1.it.

For an extracurricular internship, just send the extracurricular internship report (template on the SMA Internships page).
