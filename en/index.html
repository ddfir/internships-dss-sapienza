How to do an internship

Five steps, from choosing a host organisation to closing the internship on the TSP platform and getting your credits (CFU) recognised.

Start from step 1 I'm finishing my internship I'm a company
  1. 1

    Learn

    Which types of internship exist and which one suits you: curricular, extracurricular, abroad.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Find a host organisation, check whether it is accredited on TSP and find your academic tutor.

  3. 3

    Formalise

    Apply or self-apply on TSP and get your signed Training Project (Progetto Formativo).

  4. 4

    Do

    Carry out the internship and keep your attendance register up to date.

  5. 5

    Validate

    Register, final report, questionnaire: who does what to close the internship and obtain the credits.

Mind the timing

Start the procedure at least one month before the planned start date. For foreign organisations and those without an agreement with Sapienza it can take longer.

Before you start

You need a TSP account

The whole procedure runs on the TSP (Tirocini Sapienza) platform. Make sure you have an active account and that your CV is filled in.

Who to write to

Technical or administrative problems on TSP: the University Internship Office (Settore Orientamento al lavoro e tirocini), tirocini@uniroma1.it.

Everything else: internship.dss@uniroma1.it. See also who's who.

Are you a company?

You will find how to host interns and sign the Agreement (Convenzione) on the dedicated page. Would you like to meet our students? Write to us to take part in the next Internship Day.
