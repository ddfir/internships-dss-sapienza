Formalise

The key step is getting the Training Project (Progetto Formativo, PF): the document signed by the host organisation, the intern and the university that authorises the start of the internship.

How to apply

Once you have chosen the organisation, there are two ways, both on TSP:

Application

To an opportunity already published, from the Ricerca opportunità (opportunity search) section.

Self-application

To the organisation you have chosen or are already in contact with, from the Autocandidature (self-applications) section.

How to get the Training Project

  1. The organisation accepts your applicationand creates the Training Project with the «Avvio tirocinio diretto» (direct internship start) function.
  2. You accept the PF on TSPCheck the data, objectives, duration (days and hours) and credit recognition. Then write to the DSS delegate stating: number of credits to be validated, Sapienza tutor, whether the internship is curricular or extracurricular and whether it is linked to your thesis.
  3. Approval by the DSS delegateThe Dean's delegate for internships approves the PF on TSP, sets the maximum number of recognisable credits and names the Sapienza tutor.
  4. Signatures and activationThe PF is signed by you, by the organisation's tutor and by the delegate, who then activates the internship on TSP. The signed PF is sent as a PDF to you, to the student office and to the organisation.
Before the start, not after

The PF must reach the DSS delegate before the internship start date: Training Projects received after the start date are not signed.

How to see where you are

In «I miei tirocini» (my internships) you can see the status of the PF. When the PF is «Attivato» (activated) and the internship «Avviato» (started), you are ready to begin. At the end the status automatically changes to «Concluso» (completed).

If the organisation has no Agreement with Sapienza

It must set one up before the internship can start: the procedure is described on the page for companies, which you can forward to your contact person at the organisation.

International students and internships abroad

Under Sapienza rules the host organisation must sign an agreement with the University: this way your activity is registered, recognised and covered by insurance.

Preferred procedure

Ask the organisation to:

  • open an account on TSP;
  • select the language (IT or EN) at the top right;
  • click Accreditation Request and follow the instructions.

If this is not possible

The organisation sends a draft internship agreement to the central Internship Office (viviana.adriani@uniroma1.it, copying tirocini@uniroma1.it and internship.dss@uniroma1.it; for SMA students also sma-dss@uniroma1.it). The Office assesses whether it can be signed and who signs it (the Rector or a delegate, such as the Head of the DSS).

In some international cases a foreign organisation or intermediary (for example DEVSTAT-EMOS or ENSAI) acts as a go-between between you and the host organisation. The Department of Statistical Sciences liaises with the central Office but does not decide on agreements: it can take a long time, so start well in advance.
