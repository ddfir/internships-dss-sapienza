Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions. For the University's general FAQ see also the FAQ on curricular and extracurricular internships (in Italian).

Students

How do I get the Training Project from TSP?

You apply, the organisation accepts and creates the PF, you accept it, the DSS delegate approves it and then everyone signs. The full sequence is in Formalise.

How many credits is my curricular internship worth?

By default 3 or 6 CFU depending on the number of hours, but up to 9 can be recognised. For Official Statistics the internship is compulsory and worth 9 CFU.

What is the Internship Conclusion Statement?

It is the final report, a document prepared by the host organisation on TSP. On TSP it may appear as «Attestazione finale» and it downloads as riepilogoQuestionario-2.pdf. See Validate.

Which documents do I need at the end of the internship and who do I send them to?

Signed Training Project, approved attendance register and the organisation's final report; SMA students also their own report. They go to the recording lecturer for your programme: see the full sequence.

What goes in the student's final report (SMA)?

Follow the fixed structure of the report template (maximum 5 A4 pages).

Who records the credits?

It depends on your degree programme: see the table of recording lecturers.

What is the procedure for international and double-degree students?

The organisation must get accredited on TSP, or send a draft agreement to the central Internship Office. Details in Formalise.

What insurance cover does the intern have?

It is described on the dedicated Sapienza page.

Can the evaluation questionnaire be printed?

No. Only you can see it, not the organisation; the Internship Office uses it for overall, anonymous analyses.

Can I do an internship while on an Erasmus study exchange?

No, you cannot also do an internship.

Companies

How do I move from a JobSoul account to TSP?

There is a guided procedure for obtaining TSP credentials (PDF, in Italian).

I am a foreign company: how do I activate an internship with Sapienza?

The internship is activated on TSP through the standard procedure: accreditation, an agreement in English signed by your legal representative, and preparation of the Training Project. TSP can be switched to English and you can ask Sapienza's Internship Office for help.
