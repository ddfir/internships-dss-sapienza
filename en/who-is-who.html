Who's who

Many people are involved in the procedure. Here is what each of them does and who to contact.

ActorWhat they do
StudentFinds the organisation, self-applies on TSP, accepts the Training Project proposed by the organisation, fills in the attendance register and the final evaluation questionnaire.
Host organisation
(company or institution)		Gets accredited on TSP, proposes and signs the PF after the student and the delegate have accepted it, approves the attendance register, fills in the final report. Has an organisation tutor.
Dean's delegate for internships at the DSS
(currently M. Alfò; previously A. Di Ciaccio)		Approves the PF on TSP, sets the maximum recognisable credits, names the Sapienza tutor and enters the other University information. After the student and the organisation tutor have signed, signs and activates the internship.
Internship Committee of the Department of Statistical Sciences
(Donatella Firmani, Monia Ranalli, Giacomo Morelli, Presidents of the master's programmes and their contacts, SMA curriculum coordinators)		Point of contact for students, liaison with the central office, proposes improvements to the procedure.
Presidents of the degree programmesTeaching regulations; the final recording of credit recognition is up to them or their delegates.
Recording lecturersRecord the internships. See the list by degree programme.
DSS student office (Segreteria didattica)Possible first point of contact: forwards students' requests. didattica_dss@uniroma1.it (by appointment only, by email).
Academic tutorSapienza lecturer who follows the internship from the academic side. You give their name to the delegate when the PF is approved.
Central University Internship OfficeRuns TSP, accredits organisations and handles agreements, especially with foreign organisations.
TSP platform (Esse3/Cineca)Technical tool shared by everyone, with different functions for each role.
Foreign organisation or intermediary
(e.g. DEVSTAT-EMOS, ENSAI)		Acts as a go-between between you and the foreign host organisation in international cases.

Who to write to

Problems with TSP

Registration, CV, applications: the University Internship Office (Settore Orientamento al lavoro e tirocini), tirocini@uniroma1.it.

Questions about the DSS procedure

internship.dss@uniroma1.it (Internship Committee).

Credits and recording

The recording lecturer for your programme and the President of your degree programme.

Agreements with foreign organisations

Go through the central Internship Office. The Department of Statistical Sciences liaises but does not decide: allow plenty of time.
